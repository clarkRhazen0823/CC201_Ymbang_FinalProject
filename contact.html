Admissions Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions asked by prospective parents and students.

When does enrollment open?

Early registration begins during the second semester, and regular enrollment continues through the opening of the school year. Transferees and late enrollees can visit the Admin Office for slot availability.

Does PAPSI accept ESC / GASTPE Vouchers?

Yes! PAPSI is an ESC-FAPE GASTPE Participating School recognized by the Department of Education. Eligible Junior High School students (Grades 7 to 10) can avail of tuition subsidies through the voucher program.

What are the requirements for transferees?

Transferees must present their latest School Form 9 (SF9 / Report Card), Certificate of Good Moral Character, PSA Birth Certificate photocopy, 2 pcs 1x1 ID pictures, and pass the entrance interview. Junior high applicants also submit a drug test and medical certificate.

How can we avail of tuition discounts?

First and second honors applicants submit certified rankings from their previous DepEd-recognized school. Families enrolling 2 or more siblings simply present PSA birth certificates at the Cashier to claim discounts up to 100% (5th child free).
