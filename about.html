Our Heritage & Inspiration
"Learn to live a good life and live to serve a good cause."

The Philippine American Progress School, Inc. (PAPSI) was founded in La Paz, Iloilo City by Dr. Melquiades F. Pugne, Ed. D., with a clear vision: that education should not merely train minds for test-taking, but empower whole human beings with life-long skills, empathy, and moral grounding.

Rooted in continuous innovation and deep community values, PAPSI creates a supportive, secure, and inspiring learning atmosphere where every Papsinian progresses at their optimal rate without being held back.

Institutional Foundations

The guiding principles that steer our classrooms, curricula, and community programs.

Our Philosophy

We believe that every child is born with a Gift of Genius. We strive in every way to awaken, cultivate, and nurture this gift through thoughtful guidance, active engagement, and holistic instruction.

Our Vision

We envision the PHILIPPINE AMERICAN PROGRESS SCHOOL, INC. to develop into a progressive institution that empowers learners with life-long learning skills in a secure and fast-changing world, adopting our core values which are leadership, collaboration, and excellence.

Our Mission

To provide programs and projects that offer quality instructions and support, empower learners with useful knowledge, and instill the importance of acquiring life-long learning skills, imparting them with effective instructions and actions necessary in living a successful, productive, and fulfilling life.

General Objectives

The school believes that investment in the youth is the most valuable and lasting legacy to the nation, bringing far-reaching benefits not only to the individual but also to the family and community.

To develop and train each learner to progress continuously in learning without being held back by others who have different rates and maximize self-learning for his optimum growth and development.

The Pillars of PAPSI

Our Core Values

Three essential virtues embedded into every classroom experience, student leadership initiative, and community interaction.

Leadership

Leading the way to effect positive change and development. We encourage students to take initiative, stand for integrity, and inspire others through selfless service.

Collaboration

Working together in harmony between and among stakeholders for a common goal. We cultivate teamwork, open communication, mutual respect, and social awareness.

Excellence

Aiming for and achieving a high standard of performance in academics, personal discipline, creative endeavors, and civic involvement every single day.
