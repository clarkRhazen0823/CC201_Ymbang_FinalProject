Academic Programs & Offerings

Empowering learners with progressive curricula, specialized enrichment tracks, and values-centered instruction from early childhood to junior high school.

Core Curriculum

Regular Academic Programs

Recognized by the Department of Education Region VI, structured to inspire curiosity, character, and mastery.

Early Childhood Education

Kindergarten

Gov't Recog. (R-VI) No. ER-25 S. 1999

Inculcates the value of love for learning and work ethics through active play, fun-filled experiences, and hands-on learning tasks that nurture natural creativity.

  • Play-based sensory learning
  • Foundational phonics & numeracy
  • Social habits & emotional growth
  • Fine & gross motor skills practice
Primary & Intermediate

Elementary (Grades 1–6)

No. ER-26 S. 1999 & ER-11 S. 2001

Provides meaningful activities leading to all-around development: physical, intellectual, social, emotional, and spiritual, shaping children into harmonious and empathetic individuals.

  • Rigorous English, Math & Science
  • Filipino & Araling Panlipunan
  • Character building & spiritual values
  • Collaborative team projects
Secondary Education

Junior High (Grades 7–10)

Gov't Recog. (R-VI) No. ER-57 S. 2005

Instills self-motivational skills, self-education, and self-empowerment, promoting student welfare and providing opportunities for attaining a happy, useful, and productive life.

  • ESC-FAPE GASTPE voucher grantee
  • Scientific research & project methods
  • Leadership & campus organizations
  • High school-to-college preparedness
Enrichment & Holistic Growth

Special Development Programs

Unique academic and co-curricular programs engineered to awaken every student's talents and prepare them for real-world leadership.

Performing Arts

Enhances the students' creative and artistic talents through gross and fine motor coordination in dancing, singing, declaiming, acting, and drawing.

Excel Class

Further improves the quality of education to produce globally competitive students in the specialized fields of English, Science, and Mathematics.

Remedial Class

Enables students who need extra individualized help and reinforcement to successfully cope with and master lessons in the regular classes.

Disaster Preparedness

Promotes public safety awareness through activities and projects geared towards emergency preparedness, first aid response, and safety practices.

Special Needs Inclusion

Mainstreaming of students upon recommendation and thorough assessment from developmental psychologists, championing the school's advocacy of education for all.

Sea Scouts Program

Affiliated with the 601.14 Philippine Coast Guard Auxiliary (PCGA) of Western Visayas, providing youth training, civic assistance, and maritime stewardship.

Admission & Enrolment Requirements

PAPSI opens its doors to students who desire quality education. Admission is granted to those who present valid credentials:

Pre-Kinder & Kindergarten

  • Photocopy of NSO / PSA Birth Certificate
  • 2 pcs 1x1 ID Picture (recent)
  • Admission Test and Personal Interview (for Kindergarten)

Incoming Students (Grades 1 to 10)

  • Pass the Admission Test and Personal Interview
  • Original copy of School Form 9 (SF9 / Report Card)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • 2 pcs 1x1 ID Picture
  • Medical Certificate
  • Drug Test (for Junior High School applicants)
  • Learner's Reference Number (LRN)
