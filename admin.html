Governance & Services

Administrative Structure

Our offices work in synergy to foster a well-ordered, safe, and academically enriching environment for every Papsinian.

Office of the Principal

Academic & Institutional Head

Oversees curriculum execution, faculty development, institutional policy, and leads the preliminary interviews for new student applicants.

Office of the Registrar

Admissions & Records

Maintains official student records, Learner Reference Numbers (LRN), DepEd School Forms (SF9/SF10), transcript evaluation, and transfer credentials.

Cashier & Finance Office

Treasury & Accounts

Handles enrollment fee assessments, tuition discount grants, receipt issuance, official payment confirmations, and GASTPE/ESC vouchers.

Guidance & Counseling

Student Welfare & Inclusion

Provides emotional, social, and academic counseling. Coordinates mainstreaming assessments for students with special needs and career guidance.

Student Affairs & Discipline

Campus Culture & Safety

Fosters values formation, campus order, student governance, emergency preparedness drills, and promotes harmonious relationships among all Papsinians.

Sea Scouts Directorate

PCGA 601.14 Auxiliary Partner

Liaises directly with the Philippine Coast Guard Auxiliary (Western Visayas) for maritime safety youth training, service learning, and leadership activities.

Official Procedure

Enrolment Flowchart

Follow this step-by-step enrollment procedure to secure your child's official registration at PAPSI.

1
Principal's Office

Preliminary Interview

Preliminary interview of the applicant conducted by the School Principal to evaluate learning background and level placement.

2
Admin Office

Get Requirements List

Secure the official checklist of enrollment requirements, credentials, and admissions documentation from the Admin Office.

3
Admin Office

Submit Requirements

Submit all required credentials (NSO/PSA birth certificate, Form 137/138, ID pictures, Good Moral certificate) for review.

4
Admin Office

Fill-up Registration Form

Complete the official Student Registration Form accurately with parent/guardian contact details and student background.

5
Admin Office

Assessment & Tuition

Verify applicable discounts (honor scholarships, family discounts, cash payment discount) and determine required payment amount.

6
Cashier's Window

Secure Receipts & Payment

Pay the required enrollment fee and confirm payment with an official printed school receipt from the Cashier.

7
Cashier's Window

Get Notated Enrolment Form

Receive the official photocopy of the enrolment form duly stamped with the authorized notation "ENROLLED".

8
Homeroom / Faculty

Present to Homeroom Adviser

Present the stamped enrolment copy to the designated homeroom class adviser for sectioning, class schedules, and orientation.

Service Reminders for Visitors

To facilitate efficient processing of documents and certificates:

  • Transcript Requests: File requests for SF10 / Form 137 at least 3 to 5 business days in advance.
  • Honors & Discounts: Bring original ranking certifications and birth certificates for verification upon enrollment.
  • Campus Safety: Visitors are requested to log in at the security post and present a valid ID before entering administrative offices.
